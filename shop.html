Size Chart

Size Label Nail Width (mm) Typical Fit
XS10 – 12 mmPetite / narrow
S12 – 14 mmSmall
M14 – 16 mmMedium (most common)
L16 – 18 mmLarge
XL18 – 20+ mmExtra large / wide
