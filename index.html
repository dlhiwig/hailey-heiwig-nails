Art You Wear

Hailey creates custom long nails and press-ons that go beyond polish. Each set is built as wearable art — whether through 3D florals, chrome finishes, ombré gradients, or fully themed designs.

The emotional core is simple: nails as a small but visible piece of identity. Confidence, self-expression, and the feeling of being a bolder version of yourself.

From fully custom sculpted sets to limited press-on drops, the work is made to be seen, shared, and lived in.

What I Offer

Custom Sets

Fully sculpted long nails (acrylic, gel, polygel) with artistic detailing. In-person or coordinated process.

Press-On Drops
