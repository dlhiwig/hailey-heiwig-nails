About Hailey

Hailey Heiwig creates custom long nails and artistic press-ons that function as wearable art. Her work focuses on length, shape, and detailed design — 3D florals, chrome, ombré, and thematic sets that let people express a bolder version of themselves.

The emotional draw is identity and confidence. A set of nails can change how someone feels when they look at their hands. That small shift is what the work is built around.

Whether through fully custom sculpted sets or limited press-on drops, the goal is the same: art that lives on you and gets shared.

Open to custom commissions, press-on collections, and brand collaborations that align with the aesthetic.
